# Antarctic ambience

The mounted looping audio element plays a single synchronized mix of the two user-supplied tracks: Antarctica — Yet Another Morning at the Glacier and Antarctic Sights and Sounds. The shorter music track repeats under the 254-second nature recording. Music gain 0.35, nature gain 0.7; limiter ceiling 0.85 without auto gain, two-second entry/exit fades. MPEG audio, 44.1 kHz, 160 kbps. Browser playback gain 0.35.

This is pre-mixed so the tracks do not drift or compete for mobile audio playback. Existing shared login/portal mute preference and gesture unlock are preserved. Browsers may require the first tap/unmute before sound starts. Versioned media and script URLs avoid stale playback; MP3 stays outside the service-worker cache.
