2. Purpose of this PAIA Manual

This manual identifies records that may be available without a formal request; explains how to request access to records held by Little Feet Technologies (Pty) Ltd; describes subjects and categories of records held; identifies records maintained under applicable legislation; provides Information Officer contact details; explains how to obtain the Information Regulator's PAIA Guide; and describes relevant personal-information processing, recipients, transborder processing and general safeguards.

The Information Regulator makes available a Guide on how to use PAIA. It covers PAIA and POPIA rights and procedures, request forms, assistance, remedies, complaints, manuals, voluntary disclosure, fees and regulations. The Guide may be obtained from the Information Regulator or requested from the Information Officer. For public inspection during normal business hours, Little Feet specifies English and Afrikaans.

8. Processing of personal information

8.1 Purposes

  • Provide, administer and support the Little Feet school/ECD management platform.
  • Create/manage authorised accounts, authentication, roles and permissions.
  • Support learner administration, attendance, educational/progress records and authorised care-related workflows.
  • Enable authorised communications between institutions, staff and families.
  • Manage consent, safety and safeguarding-related platform functions where used by a client.
  • Administer billing, support, service delivery and business operations where applicable.
  • Protect the platform through security, audit, fraud-prevention and incident-management functions.
  • Comply with applicable legal, regulatory, accounting and record-keeping obligations.

8.2 Categories of data subjects

Learners/children; parents, guardians and authorised family contacts; school/ECD staff, principals and authorised users; client representatives; Little Feet personnel; suppliers/service providers; website/platform users and other persons whose information is legitimately processed through the service. Information may include identity/contact information, account and role information, attendance and educational records, authorised care/health-related information, consent choices, communications, uploaded records, billing/transaction information, and technical/audit information as applicable.

8.3 Recipients

Information may be supplied, where lawful and necessary, to authorised school/ECD clients and personnel; parents/guardians and other authorised users; cloud hosting/storage and technical providers; email/communications providers; professional advisers and contracted service providers subject to appropriate obligations; and regulators, courts, government or law-enforcement bodies where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

8.4 Planned transborder flows

Little Feet Technologies (Pty) Ltd may use cloud infrastructure and service providers whose systems may process or store personal information outside South Africa. Where cross-border processing occurs, Little Feet applies POPIA requirements, including applicable safeguards governing transborder flows. Specific locations may depend on infrastructure and service-provider configuration in use from time to time.

8.5 General security measures

Little Feet applies reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the platform and information processed. Measures implemented or maintained, as applicable, include authentication and controlled account access; role-based and institution/tenant-scoped authorisation; server-side access controls; restricted private-file access; session/application security; input, API and workflow validation; logging/audit/security monitoring capabilities; reasonable controls around imports, exports and administrative operations; managed hosting/storage safeguards; and incident-response, access-restriction and account-protection processes. Security-sensitive implementation details are not published in this manual.
