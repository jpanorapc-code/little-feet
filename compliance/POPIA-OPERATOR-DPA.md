# POPIA Operator / Data Processing Addendum

This addendum is intended to accompany a Little Feet school/ECD client agreement. It must be completed with the client identity and commercial agreement details before signature.

## 1. Parties and roles
The client school/ECD provider ("Responsible Party") determines the purpose and means of processing its learner, guardian, staff and school records. Little Feet Technologies (Pty) Ltd ("Operator") processes such personal information only with the knowledge or authorisation of the Responsible Party, except where Little Feet acts separately as a responsible party for its own lawful corporate/service administration.

## 2. Instructions and purpose
The Operator will process client personal information only for the contracted Little Feet services and documented lawful instructions. The Responsible Party remains responsible for establishing the lawful basis and any additional authorisation required for children's or special personal information.

## 3. Confidentiality and access
The Operator will ensure persons authorised to process client personal information are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations and access is limited to legitimate role and service purposes.

## 4. Security
The Operator will establish and maintain reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to foreseeable risks, including access control, tenant separation, secure sessions, private-file controls and appropriate security testing. Security measures will be reviewed and updated where necessary.

## 5. Security compromise
If the Operator has reasonable grounds to believe client personal information has been accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person, the Operator will notify the Responsible Party immediately and provide available information reasonably necessary for the Responsible Party's POPIA section 22 duties. The Responsible Party controls statutory notification to the Information Regulator and affected data subjects unless the law requires otherwise.

## 6. Sub-operators and infrastructure
The Operator may use infrastructure and service providers necessary to deliver the service. Little Feet will maintain a vendor/operator register and require appropriate confidentiality/security protections. Material provider changes affecting personal information will be assessed.

## 7. Cross-border processing
Where personal information is transferred outside South Africa, the parties will ensure an applicable POPIA section 72 basis exists. Little Feet will not represent an unknown hosting/storage location as South African.

## 8. Data-subject requests
The Operator will reasonably assist the Responsible Party with requests concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction/objection or other applicable rights for data held on the Responsible Party's behalf. Little Feet will not disclose another person's information without appropriate authority.

## 9. Retention, return and deletion
Client personal information will be retained only while authorised by the contracted purpose, applicable law, legitimate evidentiary requirements or other lawful basis. On termination or a valid deletion instruction, data will be returned/deleted/de-identified as applicable, subject to lawful retention and backup/recovery constraints. Retained restricted records will not be used for unrelated purposes.

## 10. Audit and assistance
Little Feet will maintain reasonable evidence of its relevant safeguards and provide reasonable compliance information needed by the Responsible Party, subject to confidentiality and security restrictions.

## 11. Responsibility
Each party remains responsible for its own POPIA duties. This addendum does not transfer the Responsible Party's duty to determine lawful purposes/instructions to the Operator.
