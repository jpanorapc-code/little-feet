# POPIA Data Subject Request Forms

Use these internal forms alongside any prescribed Information Regulator form where applicable.

## LF-POPIA-01 - Objection to Processing
Use for an objection under POPIA section 11(3).

Record the data subject name, account or school reference, contact details, processing objected to, reason, supporting information, signature or recorded submission, and date.

## LF-POPIA-02 - Correction, Deletion or Destruction Request
Use for a POPIA section 24 request.

Record the data subject name, account or school reference, contact details, whether correction/deletion/destruction is requested, the affected information, requested correction or reason, supporting evidence, signature or recorded submission, and date.

Verify identity and authority proportionately before disclosure or alteration. Information may be retained where lawfully required or authorised.

## LF-POPIA-04 - Unsolicited Electronic Direct-Marketing Consent
Use only when Little Feet intends to approach a person by unsolicited electronic communication and section 69(2) requires consent.

Record the data subject, selected electronic channel, contact detail, consent decision, method of recorded consent, and date. Consent is voluntary and may be withdrawn. Marketing communications must provide an accessible opt-out.

## Internal handling
Record receipt, identity/authority verification, responsible-party/operator allocation, decision, action, response date, and any lawful refusal or retention reason.
