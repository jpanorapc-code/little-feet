# POPIA Section 57 Prior-Authorisation Screening

Run this screening **before** starting new processing that may fall within section 57.

Answer and retain evidence for each:
1. Will Little Feet process unique identifiers for a purpose other than the purpose for which the identifier was specifically intended at collection, with the aim of linking the information together with information processed by other responsible parties?
2. Will Little Feet process information about criminal behaviour or unlawful/objectionable conduct on behalf of third parties?
3. Will Little Feet process personal information for credit reporting?
4. Will Little Feet transfer special personal information or children's information to a third party in a foreign country that does not provide an adequate level of protection as contemplated by POPIA?

If **yes or uncertain**, the Information Officer must obtain legal/compliance review and determine whether prior authorisation from the Information Regulator is required **before processing starts**. Do not treat ordinary processing of children's information by itself as proof that section 57 prior authorisation is required; children's information separately requires a lawful section 35 basis/authorisation.
