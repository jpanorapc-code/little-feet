## Purpose and scope
This framework governs Little Feet Technologies (Pty) Ltd and the Little Feet platform. A school/ECD provider will ordinarily determine why and how its learner, guardian and staff records are processed and will usually be the responsible party for those records. Little Feet may act as operator on the client's instructions and separately as responsible party for its own account, billing, support, security and corporate administration.

## POPIA conditions
1. **Accountability:** the Information Officer owns this framework, the PIIA, PAIA Manual, request process, incident process, registers and review cycle.
2. **Processing limitation:** collect only adequate, relevant and non-excessive information for a defined lawful purpose. Establish the applicable lawful basis. Children's and special personal information require the additional authorisation/basis POPIA requires.
3. **Purpose specification:** record the purpose before processing. Retain only while authorised by purpose, law, contract or other lawful basis; then securely delete, destroy or de-identify.
4. **Further processing:** do not reuse school-held information incompatibly. Material new uses require review of purpose, lawful basis, notice and PIIA.
5. **Information quality:** authorised correction workflows and data-subject procedures must support reasonable accuracy.
6. **Openness:** maintain the Privacy & POPIA Notice, legal identity, Information Officer details and PAIA Manual and provide required collection notices.
7. **Security safeguards:** identify foreseeable risks, maintain reasonable technical/organisational safeguards, verify safeguards and bind operators to confidentiality/security obligations.
8. **Data-subject participation:** support access, correction/deletion where lawful, objection, consent withdrawal where applicable, complaints and PAIA access.

## Current technical evidence
The current codebase contains role-based and tenant-scoped authorisation, secure sessions, login throttling/cooldown, selected sensitive-field encryption configuration, private object-storage architecture, bounded inputs, audit/security controls, account/school deletion workflows and automated security, authorisation and tenant-isolation tests. These controls must be maintained; their existence does not replace operational compliance.

## Governance records
The Information Officer maintains:
- processing-activity register;
- PIIA and change assessments;
- data-subject/PAIA request register;
- security-compromise register;
- vendor/operator and cross-border register;
- retention schedule;
- awareness/training register; and
- periodic access/compliance review record.

New high-risk processing must be reviewed before production. No person may access personal information merely because technical access is possible; access must have a legitimate role and purpose.

## Open actions
Provider-by-provider contracts and cross-border facts must be verified before being marked complete. Retention periods must be tied to actual legal/contractual/business requirements and must not be invented. Prior-authorisation screening must occur before processing that may fall within POPIA section 57.
