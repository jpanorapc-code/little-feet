# POPIA Request and Security-Compromise Procedures

## A. Data-subject / PAIA requests
Primary channel: **Christiaan@littleFeet.co.za**

For every request record: date, requester, request type, affected account/school, identity/authority verification, responsible-party/operator allocation, decision, action, response date and reason for any refusal or retention.

1. Verify identity and authority proportionately; do not collect unnecessary identity material.
2. Identify whether Little Feet or the client school is the responsible party for the requested record.
3. For operator-held school data, coordinate with the responsible party unless law/contract requires otherwise.
4. Correct inaccurate information where authorised.
5. Delete only where lawful; preserve information subject to valid legal, contractual, safeguarding or evidentiary retention.
6. For consent withdrawal, apply it prospectively where consent is the applicable basis; document another lawful basis if processing continues.
7. For a child, verify competent-person or other applicable authority before disclosure/change.
8. Record complaints and provide the Information Regulator route where appropriate.
9. Use the PAIA process where PAIA governs access to the record.

## B. Security compromise
1. **Detect and preserve:** record discovery, affected systems/data/tenants, suspected cause and preserve relevant evidence.
2. **Contain:** revoke compromised sessions/credentials, restrict affected paths, isolate where necessary, correct misconfiguration and prevent further access.
3. **Escalate:** notify the Information Officer immediately. If Little Feet is operator, notify the responsible party immediately when the POPIA trigger is met.
4. **Assess and notify:** where Little Feet is responsible party and there are reasonable grounds to believe personal information was accessed/acquired by an unauthorised person, follow POPIA section 22 and current Information Regulator reporting requirements. Do not invent a risk threshold that POPIA does not provide.
5. **Recover:** restore integrity and safe operation.
6. **Document:** record scope, decisions, notices, remediation and lessons learned.
7. **Improve:** update safeguards, PIIA, training and vendor controls as needed.

Do not publicly disclose technical details that would create additional security risk.
