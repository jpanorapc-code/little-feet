# POPIA Awareness and Security-Compromise Training

Before a person receives access to personal information in Little Feet, cover confidentiality and least privilege; tenant/school boundaries; children's and special personal information; credential/session and phishing security; data-subject request verification; retention/deletion; secure handling of exports/files; and immediate reporting of suspected security compromises.

For a suspected compromise the trainee must understand: detect and preserve evidence, contain, immediately escalate to the Information Officer, assess responsible-party and POPIA notification duties, recover, document, and improve safeguards.

Completion must be recorded in POPIA-OPERATIONAL-REGISTERS.md. This programme does not claim future employees have completed training.
