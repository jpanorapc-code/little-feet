# Personal Information Retention Schedule

**Rule:** POPIA does not create one universal retention period. Personal information must not be kept longer than authorised/necessary, subject to applicable law, lawful business/evidentiary need, contract, consent or other lawful basis. When no longer authorised, securely delete/destroy or de-identify as soon as reasonably practicable.

| Record class | Retention rule |
|---|---|
| Active account/profile | While account/service relationship is active and necessary for service/security; then deletion/de-identification subject to lawful exceptions |
| Learner/school operational records | Client school's documented retention instruction and applicable education/legal requirements; Little Feet as operator must not invent a longer period |
| Care/medical/medication/safeguarding | Only for the authorised care/safeguarding purpose and applicable legal/evidentiary requirement; apply restricted access and review at end of need |
| Consent/authorisation evidence | While needed to prove the processing authority and for applicable dispute/evidentiary period |
| Support/communications | While needed to resolve/support the matter and any legitimate evidentiary/security requirement |
| Security/audit logs | Defined operational security period proportionate to investigation and accountability needs; Information Officer must approve the configured period |
| Billing/invoices/accounting/tax | Applicable statutory financial/tax retention period plus only what is lawfully required |
| Uploaded files/photos | Same rule as the underlying record/purpose; delete when the linked authorised purpose/retention ends |
| Data-subject/PAIA request records | While reasonably required to evidence compliance, decisions and any legal review/complaint |
| Security-compromise records | While reasonably required for regulatory, legal, security and evidentiary purposes |
| Backups | Shortest practical recovery lifecycle consistent with resilience; expired records must not be restored into ordinary use and must age out through the backup lifecycle |

## Implementation rule
No numeric retention period may be added merely for convenience. Before configuring a period, record the source: statute/regulation, client instruction, contract, documented lawful business/evidentiary need, or consent where applicable. The Information Officer approves the schedule and reviews it annually.
