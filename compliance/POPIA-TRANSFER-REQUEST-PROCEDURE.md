# Personal Information Transfer Request Procedure

This procedure covers requests asking Little Feet to provide personal information to another organisation. It does not describe POPIA as creating a universal technical portability right.

1. Record the request, records, intended recipient and requested format.
2. Verify identity and authority proportionately; for a child verify applicable lawful authority.
3. Determine Little Feet's role. For school/ECD data held as operator, coordinate with the responsible party unless law or contract authorises direct action.
4. Determine whether transfer is legally required, contractually required, or can lawfully be accommodated. Use PAIA/POPIA access processes where applicable.
5. Check third-party rights, confidentiality, safeguarding restrictions, legal holds and lawful retention.
6. If approved, export only authorised records using a reasonable secure format and transfer method. Do not expose another tenant's or person's data.
7. Record the transferred records, recipient, authority, method, date and approval.
8. If refused or limited, provide the applicable reason and complaint/review route where required.
