## Onboarding checklist
Before a provider handles personal information:
- identify exact service and data categories;
- establish responsible-party/operator relationship;
- review confidentiality/security terms;
- record breach-notification commitment;
- record deletion/return terms;
- identify subprocessors where material;
- identify processing/storage locations where available;
- document the POPIA section 72 basis for any foreign transfer;
- perform section 57 prior-authorisation screening where relevant;
- update the PIIA for material/high-risk changes.
