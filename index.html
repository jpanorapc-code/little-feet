    📥 Import School Data

    ADMIN & PRINCIPAL

    Bring an existing school register into Little Feet without manually recreating each learner. Upload an Excel or CSV export from the school system, review the detected rows, then confirm the import. Duplicate learner records are skipped and reported.

    1. Upload a register

    Supported formats: .xlsx, .xls, and .csv. Use column headings such as Learner Name, Grade/Class, Parent Name, Parent Email, Medical Notes, Emergency Contact, and Authorised Pickups.

    Paper records and scans

    For privacy, this portal does not send child records from a photo or PDF to a public AI service. Scan paper files into a spreadsheet using your school-approved OCR or scanner, then upload the reviewed Excel/CSV export here.

    Every imported learner must be reviewed before submission. Medical, emergency-contact, and pickup details are encrypted when saved.

    Duplicate protection

    Records are matched by learner name, class/grade, and parent email. A duplicate does not overwrite the existing learner record; it is listed for review after the import.

    🛡️ Safeguarding & Privacy Controls

    RESTRICTED

    Use only for authorised school duties. Confirm school policy, parental authority, and the applicable POPIA/Children’s Act process before recording sensitive information.

    Parental media consent

    Record separate choices for private class updates and optional marketing photography. No consent means no photo processing for that purpose.

    Pickup / check-in audit

    Record the responsible adult and one-time verification code at handover. Codes are stored as one-way hashes, not readable text.

    Staff child-protection checks

    Record the certificate reference and expiry only after the school has completed the official NCPR process. Never treat this screen as a substitute for a government clearance.

    Medication two-step control

    Use Care & Safety to record the parent-authorisation reference, dosage, time, administering teacher, and witness. A separate school workflow must verify the approval before administration.
